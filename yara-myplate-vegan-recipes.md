About: Enjoy this tasty fruit dish in the morning or as a snack anytime of the day.

Ingredients for Tropical Morning Treat:

- 1 apple

- 1 banana

Directions for Tropical Morning Treat:

### MyPlate recipe: Farmers Market Gazpacho | غازباتشو الخضار (شوربة باردة)
Vegan recipe: Farmers Market Gazpacho (Arabic name: غازباتشو الخضار (شوربة باردة)). Type: cold soup. Makes 4 servings; serving size: 1/4 of recipe.
Nutrition per serving (USDA panel): 138 kcal, 5.4 g protein, 7.7 g fiber.
About: Garlic, cumin, and lemon juice give a zesty flavor to this cold, blended vegetable soup. Cilantro added at the end leaves a refreshing pop of flavor.

Ingredients for Farmers Market Gazpacho:
- 2 medium cucumbers, diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 3 medium red bell peppers, seeded and diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 3 medium green peppers, seeded and diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 4 medium celery stalks, diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 2 medium tomatoes, diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 1 medium onion, diced into 1/4 inch pieces
- 2 cups low-sodium tomato juice
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- salt and pepper (to taste, optional)
- 2 lemons, juiced

Directions for Farmers Market Gazpacho:
1. Wash hands with soap and water.
2. Combine all ingredients except salt, pepper, and lemons in a bowl.
3. Remove 2 cups of the mixture and reserve.
4. Using a blender or food processor, puree the remaining mixture in the bowl.
5. Add 2 cups of reserved mixture to the pureed mixture.
6. Season with salt, pepper (optional) and the juice from the lemons.
7. Cover mixture and refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
8. Serve cold, garnished with chopped cilantro.

Source: recipe by Veggin' Out Recipe Book, Johnson and Wales University and Rhode Island Department of Health WIC Program. Published by the U.S. Department of Agriculture on MyPlate Kitchen (https://www.myplate.gov/recipes/farmers-market-gazpacho), public domain. Ingredients for Rise and Shine Cobbler:

- 1 cup pear halves (canned, drained and sliced)
- 6 prunes (pitted, each cut in half)
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 orange
- 1 cup granola, low-fat

Directions for Rise and Shine Cobbler:
1. Wash hands with soap and water.

5. Add orange peel and juice to fruit mixture.
6. Stir.
7. Top with granola.
8. Microwave on high for 5 minutes.
9. Let stand for 2 minutes.
10. Spoon into 4 bowls and serve warm.

### MyPlate recipe: Vegetarian Chili | تشيلي نباتي بالخضار والفاصوليا
Vegan recipe: Vegetarian Chili (Arabic name: تشيلي نباتي بالخضار والفاصوليا). Type: main dish. Makes 4 servings; serving size: 1/4 of recipe.
Nutrition per serving (USDA panel): 276 kcal, 11.5 g protein, 14.5 g fiber.
About: This delicious chili packed full of veggies, beans, and spices is fast enough for a weeknight dinner. Top with cilantro for a boost of flavor.

Ingredients for Vegetarian Chili:
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (or cooking oil of choice)
- 2 large onions, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 1 medium green bell pepper, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 jalapeno chilis, diced
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 cup cilantro, chopped, divided
- salt and pepper (to taste, optional)
- 2 cans (14.5 ounces each) low-sodium whole tomatoes, cut into 1/4-inch pieces (or 8 medium fresh tomatoes)
- 2 medium zucchini, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 2 medium summer squash, cut into 1/4-inch pieces
- 1 can (15.5 ounces) low-sodium kidney beans, rinsed

Directions for Vegetarian Chili:
1. Wash hands with soap and water.
2. Add oil to a large pot and sauté onions, bell pepper, garlic, and jalapeno over medium-high heat for about 5 minutes, stirring often.
3. Add chili powder, cumin, half of the chopped cilantro, salt, and pepper, and continue cooking for another 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the tomatoes, zucchini, and squash.
5. Bring mixture to a simmer.
6. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
7. Add beans, and continue to simmer for another 5 minutes.
8. Serve the mixture hot.
9. Put remaining cilantro on top.

Source: recipe by Veggin' Out Recipe Book, Johnson and Wales University and Rhode Island Department of Health WIC Program. ### MyPlate recipe: Dave's Herb-Stuffed Mushrooms | فطر محشي بالأعشاب والحمص
Vegan recipe: Dave's Herb-Stuffed Mushrooms (Arabic name: فطر محشي بالأعشاب والحمص). Type: side or appetizer. Makes 4 servings; serving size: 2 stuffed mushrooms.
Nutrition per serving (USDA panel): 135 kcal, 5.1 g protein, 3.8 g fiber.
About: Mushrooms stuffed with chickpeas, sun-dried tomatoes, herbs and spices instead of fatty, salty fillings.

Ingredients for Dave's Herb-Stuffed Mushrooms:
- 8 large mushrooms (approx. 3 inches in diameter)
- 4 cloves garlic
- 3/4 cup white onions
- 3 teaspoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon dried sage
- 1/2 teaspoon dried rosemary
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/4 cup bread crumbs (whole-wheat if available)
- 1/4 cup sun-dried tomatoes (chopped fine)
- 3/4 cup canned, drained, no-salt added chickpeas (garbanzo beans)
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- cooking spray

Directions for Dave's Herb-Stuffed Mushrooms:
1. Preheat oven to 375 °F (190 °C). Clean mushrooms with a paper towel or vegetable brush; remove stems to use for the filling.
2. While oven is warming, put mushroom caps (open side down) in oven for 10 minutes. This will prepare the caps for stuffing.
3. Chop the garlic and onions and sauté with 1 teaspoon of olive oil.
4. One minute before they are finished, add the four herbs. After 1 minute, remove from heat.
5. In a large bowl, mash mushroom stems and chickpeas.
6. Add all other ingredients. Mix well.
7. Lightly spray a baking sheet and the mushroom caps.
8. Stuff the mushrooms with the mixture and place on baking sheet.
9. Bake for 15 to 18 minutes or until the stuffing is golden brown.
10. Remove from oven, sprinkle with an herb of your choice, and enjoy.
Check the label: Bread crumbs vary by brand: some contain milk or egg.

Source: recipe by USDA Center for Nutrition Policy and Promotion (submitted by Dave, MyPlate nutritionist). Ingredients for Spring Vegetable Sauté:
- 1 teaspoon olive oil (or cooking oil of choice)

- 3 small new potatoes, quartered

- 3/4 cup asparagus pieces

6. Now add the asparagus, peas, radishes, salt, pepper, and dill.
7. Cook, stirring often, until just tender, about 4 minutes more.
8. Serve immediately.

### MyPlate recipe: Quinoa and Black Bean Salad | سلطة الكينوا والفاصوليا السوداء
Vegan recipe: Quinoa and Black Bean Salad (Arabic name: سلطة الكينوا والفاصوليا السوداء). Type: main salad. Makes 6 servings; serving size: 1 cup (254g).
Nutrition per serving (USDA panel): 167 kcal, 7 g protein, 8 g fiber.
About: Quinoa (pronounced "KEEN-wah") is a whole grain with origins in South America. It is combined in this recipe with black beans, savory vegetables, and spices for a cold salad that is light and refreshing. This salad is high in protein and can be served as a main or side dish.

Ingredients for Quinoa and Black Bean Salad:
- 1/2 cup quinoa (dry)
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 teaspoons lime juice
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon coriander (ground, dried cilantro seeds)
- 2 tablespoons cilantro (chopped)
- 2 scallions (medium, minced)
- 1 can black beans, low-sodium (15.5 ounce can, rinsed and drained)
- 2 cups tomato (chopped)
- 1 red bell pepper (medium, chopped)
- 1 green bell pepper (medium, chopped)
- 2 green chiles (minced, to taste)
- black pepper (to taste)

Directions for Quinoa and Black Bean Salad:
1. Wash hands with soap and water.
2. Rinse the quinoa in cold water. Boil water in a saucepan, and then add the quinoa.
3. Return to boil, and then simmer until the water is absorbed, 10 to 15 minutes. Cool for 15 minutes.
4. While quinoa is cooking, mix olive oil, lime juice, cumin, coriander, chopped cilantro, and scallions in a small bowl, and set aside.
5. Combine chopped vegetables with the black beans in a large bowl, and set aside.
6. Once quinoa has cooled, combine all ingredients and mix well.
7. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.

### MyPlate recipe: Peanut Stew | يخنة الفول السوداني بالبطاطس
Vegan recipe: Peanut Stew (Arabic name: يخنة الفول السوداني بالبطاطس). Type: main dish. Makes 6 servings; serving size: 1 serving.
Nutrition per serving (USDA panel): 353 kcal, 12 g protein, 7 g fiber.
About: A hearty stew combining creamy peanut butter, hearty vegetables, and spices. Perfect for a chilly evening, it brings the warmth of garlic, ginger, and cumin to your table for a satisfying meal.

Ingredients for Peanut Stew:
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup tomato paste
- 1 can diced tomatoes (about 15 oz)
- 4 cups low sodium vegetable broth
- 2 pounds whole yellow or red potatoes, peeled, halved, and cut into quarters (about 6 potatoes)
- 2 cups kale, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup peanuts, chopped
- 1 whole lime, zest and juice

Directions for Peanut Stew:
1. Heat oil over medium heat in a pot and add onions. Cook for a few minutes.
2. Add ginger and garlic. Cook for 1-2 minutes. Add cumin and salt.
3. Add peanut butter, tomato paste, diced tomatoes, and vegetable broth.
4. Bring to a boil and add potatoes.
5. Simmer for 15-20 minutes or until potatoes are fork-tender and cooked through.
6. Add kale, and continue to cook for 1-2 minutes.
7. Serve garnished with lime zest, lime juice, and crushed peanuts; drizzle with Sriracha for a spicy kick (optional).

Yara note: From the USDA page: yellow and red potatoes stay fork tender in this stew; russet potatoes may fall apart.
Check the label: Vegetable broth and stock cubes vary by brand: some contain chicken fat or milk.

Ingredients for Curried Squash Stew:

- 1 yellow onion (peeled and chopped)
- 2 cloves garlic (peeled and minced)
- 1 celery stalk (including leaves, chopped)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 large zucchini (or 2 small)
- 2 tablespoons curry powder

3. Add the butternut squash, tomatoes and chickpeas and cover. Continue cooking about 10 minutes.
4. Serve with cooked brown rice.
