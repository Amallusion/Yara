### About Yara's ingredients guide | عن دليل مكونات يارا
This guide describes the plant-based ingredients Yara tracks in Saudi supermarkets: Arabic and English names, protein, how to cook them, what to check on the label, which stores sell them, and which Yara recipes use them.
This guide has no prices. Prices come only from Yara's live price list. If the live list has no price for something, say so.
Protein values are typical values in grams per 100 g as sold (per 100 ml for milks); labels vary a little by brand.

### Plant foods with the most protein per 100 g | أغنى الأطعمة النباتية بالبروتين
Typical grams of protein per 100 g as sold (dry weight for beans, lentils and grains):
- Pumpkin seeds (shelled) (بذور اليقطين المقشرة): 30 g

### Black-eyed beans | لوبيا
Ingredient: Black-eyed beans (لوبيا). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: لوبيا ناشفة.
Protein: 23 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use black-eyed beans: Cook without soaking in 40 to 50 minutes, or about 30 minutes after a few hours of soaking.
Where Yara has found black-eyed beans: LuLu.

### Brown lentils (whole) | عدس بني (حب)
Ingredient: Brown lentils (whole) (عدس بني (حب)). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: عدس حب، عدس أخضر.
Protein: 24 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use brown lentils (whole): Whole lentils that hold their shape, for kabsa, mujaddara and salads. No soaking. Simmer 25 to 30 minutes.
Where Yara has found brown lentils (whole): Carrefour, LuLu.
Yara recipes that use brown lentils (whole): Lentil kabsa (كبسة العدس), Mujaddara with cucumber salad (مجدرة مع سلطة الخيار), Spiced lentil wraps (لفائف العدس المتبّل).

### Chickpeas (canned) | حمص معلب
Ingredient: Chickpeas (canned) (حمص معلب). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: حمص معلب.
Protein: 5 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use chickpeas (canned): Ready to use. Drain and rinse to cut the salt. A 380 to 400 g can gives roughly 240 g of chickpeas.
Where Yara has found chickpeas (canned): LuLu.
Yara recipes that use chickpeas (canned): Chickpea kabsa bowl (طبق الحمص بنكهة الكبسة), Roasted vegetable tahini wrap (لفائف الخضار المشوية بالطحينة), Freekeh with chickpeas and parsley (فريكة بالحمص والبقدونس), Smooth hummus (حمص بالطحينة), Crunchy roasted chickpeas (حمص محمص مقرمش), Chickpeas and spinach sauté (حمص مع السبانخ).

### Chickpeas (dry) | حمص حب
Ingredient: Chickpeas (dry) (حمص حب). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: حمص ناشف.
Protein: 20 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use chickpeas (dry): Soak 8 to 12 hours (a pinch of baking soda helps), then simmer 60 to 90 minutes, or about 30 minutes in a pressure cooker. Much cheaper than canned; cook a big batch and freeze it.
Where Yara has found chickpeas (dry): Carrefour, LuLu.

### Dried green peas | بازلاء خضراء مجففة
Ingredient: Dried green peas (بازلاء خضراء مجففة). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: بازلاء ناشفة.
Protein: 24 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use dried green peas: Whole dried peas: soak overnight, then simmer 45 to 60 minutes. Split peas need no soaking and take 30 to 40 minutes. Check which one the pack holds.
Where Yara has found dried green peas: LuLu.

### Foul medames (canned) | فول مدمس معلب
Ingredient: Foul medames (canned) (فول مدمس معلب). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: فول معلب، فول مدمس.
Protein: 5.5 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use foul medames (canned): Cooked fava beans. Warm with garlic, lemon, cumin and tahini for breakfast. Rinse if you want less salt.
Where Yara has found foul medames (canned): LuLu, Panda.
Yara recipes that use foul medames (canned): Foul with tahini and herbs (فول بالطحينة والأعشاب).

Where Yara has found hummus with tahini (canned): LuLu.

### Mung beans (whole) | ماش (حب)
Ingredient: Mung beans (whole) (ماش (حب)). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: ماش.
Protein: 24 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use mung beans (whole): No soaking needed, though 2 to 4 hours speeds them up. Simmer 30 to 40 minutes. Good in soups and with rice.
Where Yara has found mung beans (whole): LuLu.

### Red kidney beans (canned) | فاصوليا حمراء معلبة
Ingredient: Red kidney beans (canned) (فاصوليا حمراء معلبة). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Protein: 5 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use red kidney beans (canned): Already cooked and safe to use straight away. Drain and rinse.
Where Yara has found red kidney beans (canned): LuLu.
Yara recipes that use red kidney beans (canned): Red beans and rice (فاصوليا حمراء مع الأرز).

### Red kidney beans (dry) | فاصوليا حمراء
Ingredient: Red kidney beans (dry) (فاصوليا حمراء). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: فاصوليا حمراء ناشفة.
Protein: 23 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use red kidney beans (dry): Soak 8 to 12 hours, then boil hard for at least 10 minutes before simmering 60 to 90 minutes. Raw or undercooked kidney beans contain a natural toxin, so never slow-cook them from raw.
Where Yara has found red kidney beans (dry): LuLu.

### Red lentils | عدس أحمر
Ingredient: Red lentils (عدس أحمر). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: عدس مجروش، عدس شوربة.
Protein: 24 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use red lentils: Split, skinless lentils that cook fast and melt into soup (shorbat adas). No soaking. Simmer 15 to 20 minutes in about three times their volume of water.
Where Yara has found red lentils: Carrefour, LuLu, Panda.
Yara recipes that use red lentils: Lentil vegetable soup (شوربة العدس بالخضار).

### Split chickpeas (chana dal) | حمص مجروش (تشانا دال)
Ingredient: Split chickpeas (chana dal) (حمص مجروش (تشانا دال)). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: تشانا دال.
Protein: 20 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use split chickpeas (chana dal): Split, skinned chickpeas. Soak 30 minutes to 2 hours, then simmer 30 to 45 minutes. Good in soups and dals.
Where Yara has found split chickpeas (chana dal): LuLu.

### White beans (canned) | فاصوليا بيضاء معلبة
Ingredient: White beans (canned) (فاصوليا بيضاء معلبة). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Protein: 6 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use white beans (canned): Drain and rinse. Good in salads and quick stews.
Where Yara has found white beans (canned): LuLu.

### White beans (dry) | فاصوليا بيضاء
Ingredient: White beans (dry) (فاصوليا بيضاء). Category: Beans and lentils | البقوليات.
Also called: فاصوليا بيضاء ناشفة.
Protein: 22 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use white beans (dry): Soak 8 to 12 hours and simmer 60 to 90 minutes until creamy. Classic in tomato stews.

### Basmati rice | أرز بسمتي
Ingredient: Basmati rice (أرز بسمتي). Category: Grains and pasta | الحبوب والمعكرونة.
Protein: 7.5 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use basmati rice: Rinse until the water runs clear. About 1 cup rice to 1½ cups water, 15 to 20 minutes, then rest 5 minutes.
Where Yara has found basmati rice: Panda.
Yara recipes that use basmati rice: Lentil kabsa (كبسة العدس), Chickpea kabsa bowl (طبق الحمص بنكهة الكبسة), Mujaddara with cucumber salad (مجدرة مع سلطة الخيار), Red beans and rice (فاصوليا حمراء مع الأرز).

### Bulgur | برغل
Ingredient: Bulgur (برغل). Category: Grains and pasta | الحبوب والمعكرونة.
Also called: برغل.
Protein: 12 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use bulgur: Fine bulgur: soak in boiling water for 10 to 15 minutes (for tabbouleh). Coarse bulgur: simmer in twice its volume of water for 12 to 15 minutes.
Where Yara has found bulgur: Carrefour.

### Freekeh | فريكة
Ingredient: Freekeh (فريكة). Category: Grains and pasta | الحبوب والمعكرونة.
Also called: فريك.
Protein: 12 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use freekeh: Roasted green wheat with a smoky taste. Cracked freekeh takes about 2½ times its volume of water and 20 to 25 minutes; whole freekeh about 45 minutes.
Where Yara has found freekeh: Carrefour.
Yara recipes that use freekeh: Freekeh with chickpeas and parsley (فريكة بالحمص والبقدونس).

### Quinoa | كينوا
Ingredient: Quinoa (كينوا). Category: Grains and pasta | الحبوب والمعكرونة.
Protein: 14 g per 100 g (typical value).
How to use quinoa: Rinse first to remove the bitter coating. One part quinoa to two parts water, 15 minutes, then rest 5.
Where Yara has found quinoa: Carrefour.

### Check the label for animal ingredients | اقرأ المكونات
Most foods in this guide are plant-based by nature. These vary by brand, so check the ingredients:
- Bread and tortillas: milk or milk powder.
- Pasta: egg, especially fresh pasta.
- Peanut butter and spreads: honey or whey.
- Stock cubes and spice mixes: chicken fat or chicken flavor, common in kabsa seasonings.
- Sweets and jellies: gelatin.
- Words that mean an animal ingredient: whey, casein, lactose, milk solids, ghee or samn, gelatin, albumin (egg), carmine or E120, shellac or E904.

### Nutrients to plan for on a vegan diet | عناصر غذائية تحتاج تخطيطًا
- Vitamin B12 isn't reliably found in plant foods. Get it from fortified plant milk or cereal, or a supplement.
- Vitamin D deficiency is common in Saudi Arabia, plant-based or not. A doctor can test for it.
- Iron from beans and lentils is absorbed better with vitamin C, so the lemon and tomato in Saudi cooking help.
- Calcium: tahini, almonds, and calcium-fortified plant milk. Omega-3: chia seeds and walnuts.
- This is general information, not medical advice.
